ATCAP Senior Capstone

Led a five-person team delivering a reliable website, structured job application form, and automated review script for a home healthcare client.

Team leadPythonSquarespaceBusiness analysis
Live site View on GitHub
Client: ATCAP Home Healthcare Solutions, a home health agency in Woodbridge, VA with 31 employees. Team: M&T Solutions, five people. My role: Team Lead.

The business problems

AreaBefore
WebsiteThe WordPress site often returned a fatal error (about 25% uptime). Visitors had to call the office to learn more, costing the receptionist up to about 60 minutes.
Job applicationsApplicants emailed a resume or walked in. There was no structured way to apply.
Application reviewThe owner reviewed applications by hand, one email at a time, which was repetitive and made it easy to miss new talent.

What we delivered

  • A reliable website on Squarespace.
  • A structured application form so every application arrives in the same format.
  • An automated review process: a Python script pulls applicant data from Google Sheets, sorts it by position, and generates structured documents for the owner to review.

How it helped the business

MeasureBeforeAfter
Website uptime~25%~99.9%
Website monthly cost$248.33$23.00
Time on website-error callsup to ~60 minnone
Application intake~1 min, unstructured email~0.5 min, structured form
Application review~35 min~30 min (estimate from two applications)

Security and testing

  • Input validation, HTTPS with SSL certificate verification, user authentication, and multi-factor authentication.
  • Alpha, Beta, and user acceptance testing. A link failure found in Alpha was fixed and retested, and a script failure found in UAT (Python had to be installed on the office computer) was resolved and confirmed by the sponsor.
  • A training session and training materials so the owner can run the process independently.

My role as Team Lead

  • Led the team across two semesters, including pivots in scope and approach.
  • Was the main point of contact with the sponsor and kept her needs at the center of the work.
  • Presented the original business process analysis: how the website, application, and review processes worked and what they cost in time and money.
  • Defined the areas for improvement and presented the overview of the three technical solutions.
